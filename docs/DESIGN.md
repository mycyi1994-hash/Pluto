# Pluto design

Audience: people who want a transparent basket of Stock Tokens and a verifiable explanation of its value.

Palette: ink #10233f, paper #f4f7fb, water #dce9f5, blue #2464dc, apricot #f3a55a, green #13775d. Typography: Segoe UI for clear interface text and large tightly set product headings; tabular numerals for portfolio quantities. A left rail anchors a wide composition diagram, a constituent table and a single create/redeem panel. Mobile moves navigation above the content.

The distinctive element is the weighted orbital basket diagram: it explains composition, without inventing a historical price chart. Avoid generic metric-card grids. Left-align functional text, use generous whitespace and visibly separate live market evidence from testnet settlement. Respect focus states, small screens and reduced motion.
